WC 2026 Prediction Notifier

Get a Telegram message every time a match ends — with your prediction result.

How to set up

1
Open a chat with @mumuporra_bot on Telegram and send /chatid — it replies with your numeric ID.
2
Fill in the form below and upload your Excel predictions file. The bot will confirm by sending your full predictions list.
The file stays on the server only to read your predictions — it is not shared with anyone.
